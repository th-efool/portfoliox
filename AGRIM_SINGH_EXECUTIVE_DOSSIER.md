## 🛠️ 3. ARCHITECTURAL CASE STUDIES

### Case Study A: 9-Agent Conference Intelligence Engine (Pinch Hackathon — 1st Runner-Up)
- **The Core Problem:** High-stakes conference planning is crippled by isolated silos: sponsors live in one sheet, speaker availability in another, budget models are blind to venue constraints, and GTM is tacked on as an afterthought.
- **System Architecture:** Engineered a **directed typed state machine** where 9 discrete agents collaborate over shared, immutable state deltas in under 3 minutes:
  - **Researcher Agent:** Seeds execution by grounding the pipeline with live-scraped conference intelligence and verified industry pricing.
  - **Orchestrator Agent:** Injects macro constraints and configures downstream agents dynamically based on event scale and geography.
  - **Concurrent Domain Agents (Sponsor, Speaker, Venue):** Fire in parallel, each writing strictly scoped diffs back to the centralized typed state.
  - **Pricing Agent:** Intercepts venue costs directly from the shared state to calculate budget burn dynamically without manual reconciliation.
  - **GTM & Ops Agents:** Synthesize upstream outputs into actionable launch roadmaps and logistical checklists.
  - **Critic Agent (Autonomous QA):** Computes strict quality scores (0–10) across all outputs; selectively re-triggers upstream agents if score falls below 6.
- **Tech Stack:** `LangGraph`, `FastAPI`, `Next.js`, `Groq (LLaMA-3.3-70B)`, in-memory NumPy vector RAG with HuggingFace sentence embeddings.

### Case Study B: High-Throughput Real-Time Moderation Engine (Tinkering Lab)
- **The Core Problem:** Traditional moderation relies on slow, monolithic LLM inference that introduces unacceptable latency and non-deterministic false positives.
- **System Architecture:** Designed a **deterministic pipelined multi-agent pipeline**:
  $$\text{Input Stream} \longrightarrow \boxed{\text{Detector}} \longrightarrow \boxed{\text{Validator}} \longrightarrow \boxed{\text{Analyzer}} \longrightarrow \boxed{\text{Action Engine}}$$
  - **In-Memory Fast Processing:** Sub-millisecond pre-filtering and token sanitization.
  - **Deterministic Risk Routing:** Contextually routes ambiguous edge cases through targeted verification agents while instantly dispatching low-risk traffic.
  - **Decoupled Action Engine:** Executes rate-limiting, shadow-banning, or administrative escalation asynchronously.

### Case Study C: Autonomous Market Regime Trading Agent (PancakeSwap — 1st Runner-Up Solo)
- **The Core Problem:** Most algorithmic trading systems fail because they execute static rules in a market with non-stationary regimes (trending, mean-reverting, volatile expansion).
- **System Architecture:** Built an adaptive 5-stage continuous decision loop centered on **"Intelligent Abstention"**:
  1. **Market Intelligence Layer:** Ingests live orderbook/on-chain telemetry, maintaining normalized state.
  2. **Regime Classifier:** Classifies active macroeconomic state before any strategy fires.
  3. **Hypothesis Evaluation Layer:** Simultaneously back-tests competing strategy hypotheses and ranks candidates by expected value (EV).
  4. **Autonomous Risk Veto Agent:** Evaluates liquidity depth and drawdown risk with absolute veto power over execution.
  5. **Portfolio Feedback Layer:** Closes the loop by attributing outcome attribution back into strategy weighting.
- **Tech Stack:** `Node.js`, `TypeScript`, `Next.js` dashboard, deployed on `Railway` with 2s polling intervals.

### Case Study F: In-Vitro Immunology Research (Peripheral Blood Monocytes to Macrophages)
- **The Research Focus:** Generating and characterizing human peripheral blood monocyte-derived macrophages in vitro, under the guidance of **Prof. Pranita P. Sarangi**, **Dr. Prerna Sharma**, and **Miss Divya Singh**.
- **The Core Problem:** Understanding macrophage development and function is critical for evaluating immune responses, drug effects, and disease mechanisms, requiring highly controlled in-vitro derivation from human peripheral blood.
- **Process & Architecture:**
  1. **Isolation:** Extracted monocytes from human peripheral blood utilizing density gradient centrifugation and advanced separation techniques.
  2. **Differentiation:** Cultured isolated monocytes and induced targeted differentiation into macrophages using specific cytokines (M-CSF and IL-4).
  3. **Characterization:** Assessed morphological, phenotypic, and functional traits through flow cytometry, immunofluorescence (e.g., Gram staining of macrophages with ingested *S. epidermidis* bacteria), and functional assays including phagocytosis and cytokine production analysis.
- **Significance:** The findings pave the way for novel therapies targeting macrophage-related diseases, bridging cellular biology with potential systemic healthcare solutions.
